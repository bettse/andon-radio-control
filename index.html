Heads up: you’re viewing this over HTTPS, and browsers block HTTPS pages from reaching a ws:// device on your network. Your radio can’t serve HTTPS, so to actually control it, run this app over http on your LAN — e.g. from the folder: python3 -m http.server 9000, then open http://<your-computer-ip>:9000.
